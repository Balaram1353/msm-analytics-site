Case Studies

Real problems we've helped clients solve

A sample of recent engagements — from BI dashboards to production-scale predictive models.

MSIG

Interactive Competitive Analysis Dashboard

Built a Power BI dashboard that lets MSIG's team explore competitors, clusters, and product performance interactively. Dynamic slicers and auto-surfaced comparisons replaced hours of manual filtering with a self-serve, app-like tool.

QuantivRisk

Probabilistic Accident-Cause Analysis

Developed a Bayesian model for QuantivRisk that scores the probability each candidate factor — like excessive speed — contributed to a vehicle accident, using pre-accident telemetry. Gives analysts an explainable breakdown instead of a single black-box prediction.

Zywave

Multi-State Insurance Premium Prediction

Built a segmented premium-prediction system for Zywave — three state-specific models plus one general model — to capture regional pricing patterns a single nationwide model would average away, achieving 19–30% MAPE across all four. We also bring hands-on AWS expertise, including training and deploying models like this through SageMaker.
