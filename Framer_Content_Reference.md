## Case Studies
- **Eyebrow:** Case Studies
- **Headline:** Real problems we've helped clients solve
- **Subtext:** A sample of recent engagements — from BI dashboards to production-scale predictive models.

**Client: MSIG**
**Interactive Competitive Analysis Dashboard**
Built a Power BI dashboard that lets MSIG's team explore competitors, clusters, and product performance interactively. Dynamic slicers and auto-surfaced comparisons replaced hours of manual filtering with a self-serve, app-like tool.

**Client: QuantivRisk**
**Probabilistic Accident-Cause Analysis**
Developed a Bayesian model for QuantivRisk that scores the probability each candidate factor — like excessive speed — contributed to a vehicle accident, using pre-accident telemetry. Gives analysts an explainable breakdown instead of a single black-box prediction.

**Client: Zywave**
**Multi-State Insurance Premium Prediction**
Built a segmented premium-prediction system for Zywave — three state-specific models plus one general model — to capture regional pricing patterns a single nationwide model would average away, achieving 19–30% MAPE across all four. We also bring hands-on AWS expertise, including training and deploying models like this through SageMaker.

## FAQ
- **Eyebrow:** FAQ
- **Headline:** Common questions before you book a call
- **Subtext:** Can't find your question here? Ask it directly on the call — there's no such thing as a dumb one.

**Q: We don't have a dedicated data team — can you still help us?**
A: Yes — most of our clients don't have one either. We work with the data and tools you already have, and we're deliberate about building systems your existing team can actually maintain without hiring a data scientist.

**Q: How is this different from just hiring a freelance developer?**
A: A freelancer builds what you ask for. We start by figuring out what's actually worth building — through a structured audit and prioritization process — so you're not paying to automate the wrong thing.

**Q: How long does a typical engagement take?**
A: It depends on scope, but most projects move from discovery to a working system in 4–8 weeks. We'll give you a specific timeline after the initial strategy call, not before.

**Q: What does the first strategy call actually involve?**
A: A focused conversation about where you're losing time or money to manual work, and an honest read on whether AI or automation is actually the right fix. No sales pitch, no obligation.

**Q: Do you build custom AI models, or use existing tools?**
A: Both — whichever gets you a reliable result faster. Sometimes that's wiring together tools you already pay for; sometimes it's a custom model. We'll always tell you which, and why.
